Review and recommendation

One visual language, used at three scales.

What the current pages already do well

All 13 pages share a stable white reading surface, navy headings, blue navigation, clear page maps and diagrams. Discovery already connects AI research, prioritisation and concept iteration to the relevant activities. Implementation distinguishes execution, independent checks and accountable decisions.

The design should build on that structure. The main gap is recognition: AI-specific passages often use the same headings and paragraph treatment as general guidance.

What needs a distinct meaning

Teal means “AI can help here”
Use a dark teal rule, a pale teal surface and the literal label “How AI can help”. The framed AI monogram is optional; the text label carries the meaning.
The existing colours keep their jobs
Blue remains navigation and human decision emphasis. Indigo continues to identify examples. Green, amber and red keep their status meanings. Teal never means passed, approved or implemented.
Chapter orientation

A compact “How AI can help in this chapter” strip after the existing page map, listing relevant contributions. In a rollout, each item links to its section; it does not create a second table of contents.

Help within a section

A pale panel immediately after the activity it supports. Name the benefit, permitted inputs, AI action, human check and retained artefact. Use this where a task needs explanation.

Inline help

A slim rule and text label for a short tip or reminder. Use one sentence when the surrounding section already explains the inputs and checks.

Keep the meaning clear as the pattern expands

  • AI assistance and AI product behaviour: reserve the teal contribution treatment for AI helping delivery work. Label guidance about AI inside the delivered product “When your product uses AI”, using the existing neutral reference treatment.
  • Meaning before decoration: apply the treatment to a bounded contribution, rather than every mention of AI or an entire chapter. Avoid duplicating AI guidance in a second section.
  • Benefit without invented results: describe the work AI helps with. Any speed, quality or cost improvement is a hypothesis to measure locally.
  • Progressive detail: keep the contribution and human check visible. Collapse optional task briefs, examples and tool-specific instructions; link Eitri commands back to their existing procedures.
  • Accessible recognition: pair teal with a text label, preserve blue focus rings and underlined links, and stack input/action/review rows on mobile. Use a rule and label in print so colour is optional.

Suggested first application

Start with the existing discovery research guidance, one implementation stage, one concept and one transformation pillar. Check whether readers can identify the AI task, its evidence and the human decision without relying on colour. Extend the pattern after those examples read naturally within their chapters.

Basis: the 13 pages in site/dist, the shared styles, design.md and writing.md. This proposal illustrates presentation and editorial structure; it does not establish EDS policy or implementation status.
