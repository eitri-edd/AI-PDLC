## Editorial decisions and verification limits

The existing-agent alternative now requires a supported integration. OIDC is a documented capability, not turnkey setup. A merge may itself trigger deployment: the proposed separate human release authority must be enforced, and preventing self-review does not by itself make the approver different from the merger. Quick Spec has no approval gates for the documented feature flow. Kiro model-management and Auto restrictions are recommendations grounded in the two Kiro sources; verify the deployed settings, inference geography and actual routing. Graph terminology is inconsistent in the Strands source, so do not assume every path is deterministic.

The source notes distinguish source findings from proposed safeguards. Review workflow, CODEOWNERS patterns, permissions, bypasses, environment and plan coverage in the actual repository. No local pilot, deployment, benchmark, security test or representative-reader session was performed. Author reread assessed the changed passages against writing.md; offline tests assess structure and conventions, not factual accuracy, browser layouts or comprehension.
